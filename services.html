Services built around how people actually move

Every move is scoped to the job rather than sold as a package. Below is what each service actually covers, and the things that change what it costs — so you can tell in advance which parts you need.

In detail

What each service covers

Most quotes are a combination of two or three of these rather than one. You are not obliged to take the whole list.

Home shifting

A full household move, run room by room. We inventory what is going, pack it, dismantle the furniture that needs it, load, move, and rebuild at the other end against the same list.

  • Room-by-room inventory that becomes your quote
  • Beds, wardrobes and modular units dismantled and reassembled
  • Cartons labelled by room so unpacking is not a treasure hunt

What changes the price: the number of rooms, how much of the packing you want us to do, and the access at both ends.

Home shifting in detail

Office & commercial relocation

Planned around your downtime rather than ours. Workstations, server racks, records and furniture are moved in a sequence agreed in advance so the business can open on schedule.

  • Weekend and overnight slots, so staff return to a working office
  • Desks numbered and mapped to the new floor plan before the move
  • Documents moved in sealed, numbered crates

What changes the price: headcount, how much IT has to be disconnected and reconnected, and lift access in both buildings.

Office relocation in detail

Local shifting

A move within the same city, usually finished inside a single day. The vehicle is sized to your inventory so you are not paying for empty space, and the crew stays with the job from start to finish.

  • Same-day completion for most flats up to 3 BHK
  • Early starts to work around society timing restrictions
  • One crew end to end, not a handover halfway

What changes the price: floor and lift access, how far the truck can park from the door, and the volume.

Local shifting in detail

Intercity & long-distance moving

City-to-city relocation in a dedicated vehicle. Your consignment is not consolidated with other households unless you choose a shared load to bring the cost down.

  • Dedicated vehicle by default; shared part-load offered if you want it
  • Driver's contact shared with you at dispatch
  • Delivery window agreed before the vehicle leaves

What changes the price: distance, the size of the vehicle your inventory needs, and whether you share the load.

Intercity moving in detail

Car & bike transportation

Vehicles travel in a covered carrier rather than being driven across, so nothing is added to the odometer and the paint is not exposed to the road for a thousand kilometres.

  • Covered carrier, loaded and strapped at the wheels
  • Condition noted and photographed at both ends
  • Can run on the same booking as your household goods

What you need: the registration papers, and the tank at roughly a quarter full.

Vehicle transport in detail

Packing & unpacking

The part of a move that decides whether anything breaks. Fragile items are wrapped in layers and boxed by room, and every carton is labelled with its room and contents before it leaves the floor.

  • Bubble wrap, corrugated sheets and stretch film as the item needs
  • Furniture hardware bagged and taped to the item it came off
  • Unpacking and packing waste removed if you want it

Tip: having us pack only the kitchen, glass and electronics — and doing clothes and books yourself — is the single easiest way to bring a quote down.

Packing in detail

Loading & unloading

Labour only, for when you have your own vehicle or have packed yourself. Heavy items go in first and low, fragile cartons ride on top, and the load is strapped so nothing shifts in transit.

  • Trolleys and lifting straps for heavy furniture
  • Stairs and buildings without a lift planned for, not improvised
  • Loading sheet signed off with you before the vehicle moves

What changes the price: the floor, the lift, and the carry distance from door to vehicle.

Loading and unloading in detail

Storage & warehousing

For when possession dates do not line up. Goods are inventoried in, wrapped and held off the floor, then delivered out when you are ready — charged by duration and separately from the move.

  • Short and long-term, from a few days upwards
  • Inventoried in and out, so you know exactly what is held
  • Rate confirmed in writing before anything goes in

Worth knowing: tell us as early as you can if your new home may not be ready. Arranging storage in advance costs less than arranging it on the day.
